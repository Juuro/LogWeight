LogWeight Support

Need help with LogWeight? Use the support form below and we will review your request.

  • Contact email: support@logweight.app
  • Contact form: Submit a support request
  • Data location: your body-weight data lives in Apple Health. LogWeight does not keep a separate weight database.
  • Privacy policy: Read the privacy policy
  • Support instructions: Data export and deletion instructions

Support instructions

  1. Open the Health app on your device.
  2. For export, tap your profile photo, then tap Export All Health Data.
  3. For deletion, delete values in Health and revoke LogWeight access in Settings → Health.
