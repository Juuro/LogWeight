Export & Delete Your Data

LogWeight keeps no copy of your data — everything lives in Apple Health.

  1. Open the Health app on your device.
  2. For export, tap your profile photo, then tap Export All Health Data (this exports all Health data, not only LogWeight entries).
  3. For deletion, delete values in Health and revoke LogWeight access in Settings → Health.

LogWeight stores nothing of its own — your data stays in Apple Health, on your device.
